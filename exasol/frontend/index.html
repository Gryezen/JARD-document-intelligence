About JARD

Document intelligence, built for accountable decisions.

JARD processes documents, extracts structured information, identifies uncertainty and connects related documents so teams can review discrepancies before taking action.

Extract

Turn PDFs and scanned documents into structured fields and tables.

Verify

Use confidence thresholds to route uncertain information for human review.

Decide
